Poetry
March 2019

Name

What's in a name? When multiple people go by the same two or three syllables written identical in form fillables, what's in a name?
How's it said? One spelling is never wed to a corresponding pronunciation and can change with region or nation. How's it said?
What does it mean? Is there more to it than at first it may seem? Does it convey you are a blessed son or the offspring of a Joan with many wars won? What does it mean?
What if I forget? Would that make you terribly upset? It wouldn't be a purposeful diss for tone and accent make it easy to miss. What if I forget?
Anyways, what is your name? Tell me your interests, no matter cool or lame. I'm new to this place and you seem nice. Sorry to go and ask you twice, but remind me, what is your name?
