Poetry
June 2019

Found

When I first came, I felt lost. Where can I get this? How much does it cost? Travel an hour to get a D cell battery?? Saying this builds character is false flattery. Buying duffel bags worth of potatoes to arrive home with mashed tomatoes. Is this really the way it goes? This is like hiking a mountain getting attacked by crows.
Then you spend some time, make some trades, and avail yourself to provide some aid. All of a sudden a nightly dinner is cooked and that person high & low you've looked has been standing there in front of you. Their job is to do what you cannot do, in fact they're a parent of two of your favorite learners who excel in Natural Science & English... but I digress and realize how easily I got out of my mess.
Family and community are so evidently important here and make the route to success clear. There's no such thing as making it alone, reach out to others and make where you stay not just a house, but a home.
