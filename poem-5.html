Poetry
December 2018

Footrace

I want, to organize a race.
It can be 5k, it can be 3k, I want these kids to think it is okay, to throw their hands up and say, this is a beautiful day, and no one else will stand in my way.
I want, to organize a race.
This race shouldn't epitomize, the struggle present in some eyes, when maths or English isn't the way you compartmentalize, these two subjects come to demonize, the hours between 7:30 and 13:30 for an otherwise, smart and generous kid who tries.
I want, to organize a race.
For the goal is really only one, to spend a day out in the sun, breathing air and having fun, overcoming a challenge while worrying about none, but yourself and crossing that finish line to say you are done.
I want, to organize a race.
But what if no one shows to the race, or requests are ignored for the route & space, or a kid doesn't go because his shoes don't lace, even though he probably would have set the pace, or none of these reasons are the real case, and the truth I would have to face.
I, want to organize a race.
Nobody asked for one.
